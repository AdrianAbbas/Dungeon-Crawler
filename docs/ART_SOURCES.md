# Art sources

Every image in DUNGEON CRAWLER is **original and generated by code**: no photographs, no
third-party illustrations, nothing copied from the mood references (which are mood only
and are not in this repository).

## How the art is made

- **Painted scenes (the visual overhaul):** the map, the Dungeon and the Castle (all
  their places) are built in **Blender** from Python scripts, rendered as depth layers
  (sky, far, mid, fog, near) with Cycles, and painted by a post-process (denoise, haze,
  bloom, colour grade, generalised Kuwahara, brush strokes, grain). The pipeline and how
  to run it: [tools/painted/README.md](../tools/painted/README.md). The layers are in
  `assets/painted/` and ship inside the plug-in bundles (`Contents/Resources/Painted`).
- **Engraved scenes (Milestones 7 and 8):** the other locations still use the
  procedural engraving (`Source/UI/Engraving.*`, `Scenes.cpp`, `ScenePlaces.cpp`) until
  they are painted too; it is also the fallback when the painted files are missing.
- **Fonts:** Cinzel (titles) and Cormorant Garamond (labels), both under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/`, licences beside them), embedded in the plug-in.

## Public-domain engravings

None are used. The brief allows documented public-domain 19th-century engravings
(e.g. Gustave Doré on Wikimedia Commons with a PD licence tag); the procedural
renderer made them unnecessary and avoids any licensing question. If one is added
later, list it here with its source URL, author, date and licence.

## Replacing a scene with your own art (drop-in folder)

Your own or commissioned art replaces any scene, painted or engraved, without
rebuilding. Either a single picture:

    ~/Library/Application Support/DungeonCrawler/scenes/<location>/<place>.png
    ~/Library/Application Support/DungeonCrawler/scenes/map.png

or, for parallax and drifting fog, a folder of layers named like the picture:

    ~/Library/Application Support/DungeonCrawler/scenes/dungeon/treasure-room/
        back.png (or sky.png / .jpg)   the background, opaque
        far.png  mid.png  fog.png  near.png   optional, PNG with transparency

Any size with the window's 12:7 aspect (960 x 560; 1920 x 1120 looks sharp on Retina).
Names are lower case with spaces as hyphens and without apostrophes, e.g.
`scenes/dungeon/treasure-room.png`, `scenes/wizards-tower/observatory.png`,
`scenes/castle/courtyard.png` (a location's own scene uses its scene name: Corridors,
Courtyard, Cloister, Forest Edge, Cave Mouth, Village Green, Mire, Foothills, Gates,
Stairwell). Reopen the plug-in window to see it. The map's landmarks and the scenes'
hotspots stay where the built-in art has them; the moon's glow is not drawn over your
own art.
